D · Pinned plate. The plate is pinned in view while the notes scroll past it, and it changes as each section arrives — the map answers the prose instead of illustrating it. Best at carrying a sequence of claims; the most machinery, and the one where a reader who scrolls fast can outrun the thing they are being shown.
